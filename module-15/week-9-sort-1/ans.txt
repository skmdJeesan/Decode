1: Which of the following(s) is/are true about bubble sort:
options:
    (a) It is stable sort. (✔)
    (b) It has a worst case space complexity of O(n).
    (c) It involves swapping of adjacent elements. (✔)
    (d) After each iteration, the greatest element is placed at the end of the array. (✔)
answer: a, c, d
(b) is false worst case space complexity of buble sort is O(1)

2: What will the following array look like after one iteration of bubble sort [1,6,2,5,4,3].
answer: [1, 2, 5, 4, 3, 6]
